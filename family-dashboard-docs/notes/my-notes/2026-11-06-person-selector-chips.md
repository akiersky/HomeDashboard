# Calendar selectors are dropdowns

2026-11-06. Calendar's person selector stays a dropdown: one pill, defaulting to Everyone, tap to pick someone else. A row of avatar pills was tried the same day and rejected by the user. The Week / 3-Day / Month / Agenda switcher became a dropdown too, because the view rarely changes from Week.

Chores keeps its row of pills, so the person selector still has two forms. That is deliberate: kids reach their own page from those pills in one tap.
